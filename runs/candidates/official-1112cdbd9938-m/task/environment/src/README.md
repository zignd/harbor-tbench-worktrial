The JSONL fields are event_id (nonempty string), revision (positive integer), timestamp (UTC ISO-8601 ending Z), actor (UTF-8 string), source and destination (indicator strings), and signature (valid or another verdict). A duplicate has the same event_id and revision. The greatest revision is accepted; lower revisions and repeated deliveries are rejected. Risk is source class weight plus destination class weight plus revision modulo 3, with weights private=2, public=5, mapped=7, special=4, unknown=9. Severity is low for risk below 8, medium for 8 through 14, and high for 15 or more.
