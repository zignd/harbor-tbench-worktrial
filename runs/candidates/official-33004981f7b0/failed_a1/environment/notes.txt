Manifest has secret, context, rotation. Helper derives SHA256(secret, zero byte, context). Frames are little-endian uint32 length, payload, HMAC-SHA256 tag. Payload: 16-byte reference, kind, signed little-endian int64 amount, 3-byte currency, three little-endian uint16 length-prefixed fields, then transaction rotation or adjustment target reference. Canonical accepted payloads are sorted by reference and length-prefixed before SHA256. Bank CSV columns are reference,amount,currency.
