The field prime is p=2^127-1. Each share is (x,y), and the epoch scalar is the value at zero of a threshold polynomial. The commitment is SHA-256 of its decimal scalar. Event authentication is HMAC-SHA256 over big-endian uint64 epoch, big-endian uint64 sequence, the 32 raw predecessor digest bytes, big-endian uint32 account length, account UTF-8 bytes, big-endian uint32 payload length, and payload UTF-8 bytes. Checkpoint authentication is HMAC-SHA256 over ASCII checkpoint|epoch|sequence|event_id. An anchor and descendants are authoritative only when a valid checkpoint terminates that exact predecessor-linked walk.
