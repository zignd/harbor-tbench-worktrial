Incident recovery data model

The SQLite schema is described in /app/instruction.md. Numeric fields are stored as ordinary SQLite integers or decimal text where required by the field size. Export files, when present, are line-oriented mirrors and are advisory copies of the database tables. The prime field is p=2^127-1. Interpolation is performed modulo p with distinct nonzero x coordinates. Keys are 32-byte big-endian encodings of the recovered scalar, left padded with zero bytes.

A chain edge is represented by the child predecessor field containing sha256(parent event_id) in lowercase hexadecimal. The anchor names the first event, and a checkpoint authenticates the terminal event and its sequence. A valid MAC alone does not establish authority.
