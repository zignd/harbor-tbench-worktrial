MAFv2 binary streams may contain arbitrary noise between records. Each record begins with the four-byte sync word MAF2. After the sync word, the header is little-endian: uint16 modal dimension, uint16 physical dimension, uint32 sequence number, and uint32 payload length. The payload contains a row-major modal basis with modal-dimension times physical-dimension binary64 values, followed by modal-dimension binary64 eigenvalues and physical-dimension binary64 measurements. The final uint32 is CRC32C over the header and payload, excluding the sync word and CRC. Dimensions must be between 1 and 12 and the payload length must exactly equal the encoded value count times eight. For a valid frame, q[j] = sum_i basis[j,i] * x[i] and energy = sum(q[j]^2 / eigenvalue[j]). A zero eigenvalue contributes zero only when its corresponding coefficient is exactly zero; otherwise the frame is invalid. All decoded values must be finite, and positive eigenvalues are required. Output objects contain sequence, coefficients, and energy.
