RFX2 archives begin with RFX2. Every frame has an unsigned LEB128 length and CRC32. Data payloads are kind 0, group, signed sequence, signed coefficient, and signed decimal exponent. Parity payloads are kind 1, group, count, signed sequence list, XOR coefficient parity, and XOR exponent parity. Signed integers use zigzag encoding. Frames may be reordered, duplicated, corrupted, or truncated. One missing member per parity group is recoverable.
