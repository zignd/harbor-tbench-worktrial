RFX archives begin with ASCII RFX1, followed by an unsigned varint version (1) and an unsigned varint logical record count. The remainder is a sequence of frames; physical order is not significant.

A data frame is: byte D, unsigned-varint group, unsigned-varint sequence, unsigned-varint payload length, payload, four-byte little-endian CRC32 of the payload. Its payload is two signed zigzag varints: coefficient and signed decimal exponent. The value is coefficient * 10**exponent.

A parity frame is: byte P, unsigned-varint group, unsigned-varint member count, that many unsigned-varint sequence numbers, signed-varint sum of member coefficients, signed-varint sum of member exponents, followed by a CRC32 covering the parity body after P. A group has at most one unavailable data member; subtracting valid members from parity reconstructs its coefficient and exponent. CRCs are checked before any value is accepted. Duplicate valid sequence frames are equivalent copies.
