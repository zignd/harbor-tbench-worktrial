A payload is a sequence of one-byte tags followed by unsigned LEB128 values. Tags are 1 modality (1 span, 2 counter, 3 synthetic check, 4 sampled log), 2 sequence, 3 timestamp, 4 status (zero success), 5 duration, and 6 trace identifier. Tags may be reordered. Tags 2, 3, 5, and 6 use zigzag signed integers; tag 1 is unsigned. A valid record needs tags 1, 2, 3, and 4. Missing duration and trace default to zero. Duplicate (stream, normalized modality, sequence) records are suppressed before aggregation, retaining the first valid record. Malformed records are skipped. Percentiles select the ceil(p*n/100)-th one-based sorted duration, clamped to the available range.
